Mark Minervini's 8 Trend Template Rules

1. Current Price is above both the 150-day and 200-day simple moving averages (SMAs).
2. The 150-day moving average is above the 200-day moving average.
3. The 200-day moving average is trending upward for at least 1 month.
4. The 50-day moving average is above both the 150-day and 200-day SMAs.
5. Current Price is trading above the 50-day moving average.
6. Current Price is at least 30% above its 52-week low.
7. Current Price is within 25% of its 52-week high (the closer, the better).
8. Relative Strength (RS) rating is 70 or higher (80+ preferred).
